Accessibility Statement

Last updated: 6 August 2026

Expert Hardware for Wholesale and Retail Trade is committed to making our website usable by as many people as possible, including customers with visual, motor, hearing, or cognitive disabilities. This page describes what we've done and how to reach us if something isn't working for you.

What we've done

  • Pinch-to-zoom is never disabled — you can zoom in on any page for readability.
  • Product images and icon-only buttons (wishlist, close, cart, search) carry descriptive alt text and ARIA labels for screen readers.
  • All interactive controls — search, filters, forms, the cart, and checkout — can be operated with a keyboard, not just a mouse or touch.
  • The site respects your device's reduced-motion setting for animated elements where applicable.
  • Text and background colors are chosen for reasonable contrast, and the page doesn't force a color scheme your browser or OS didn't ask for.
  • The full site is available in both English and Arabic, including right-to-left layout for Arabic.
  • We've run accessibility audits (Lighthouse) against the site and address issues they surface.

Ongoing work

Accessibility is an ongoing effort, not a one-time fix — we keep auditing and improving the site as it grows (it currently lists 800+ products across dozens of categories). If you encounter a barrier anywhere on the site, we want to know about it.

Get in touch

If you have trouble using any part of this website, or have feedback on how we can make it more accessible, please contact us — we'll do our best to help and to fix the underlying issue:
